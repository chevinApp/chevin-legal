# Privacy Policy for Chevin

**Last updated: October 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Chevin ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

**1. On-Device Processing & Data Privacy**
Chevin is an AI-powered photo curation tool designed with privacy as a fundamental priority. **All photo processing, AI analysis, and machine learning models (including TensorFlow Lite and Google ML Kit algorithms) run entirely locally on your device.** 
We do not upload, store, or transmit your photos, face data, or personal metadata to any external servers or cloud services. 

**2. Permissions We Request**
To function, Chevin requires access to your device's photo gallery (e.g., `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` on Android). This permission is strictly used to display your photos within the app, analyze them locally for quality (blur, exposure, aesthetic score), and group duplicates. You retain full control, and any action to delete or move photos is initiated solely by you.

**3. Third-Party Services**
While our processing is offline, the app utilizes underlying local libraries provided by third parties:
* **Google ML Kit:** Used locally for detecting the presence of faces to ensure important photos are not mistakenly flagged as low quality. It does not identify individuals or send biometric data to Google.
* **OpenCV & TensorFlow Lite:** Used locally for image quality assessment and feature extraction.

**4. Data Retention**
Since we do not collect your personal data or photos on our servers, we do not retain any of your data. The metadata generated by the app (such as aesthetic scores) is stored locally in your device's internal database (Room) and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

**5. Changes to This Privacy Policy**
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

**6. Contact Us**
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: chevin.feedback@gmail.com
